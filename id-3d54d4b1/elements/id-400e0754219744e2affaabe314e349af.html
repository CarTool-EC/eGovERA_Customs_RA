Goods taxonomy metadata ()
Definition: Goods taxonomy metadata ABB is DataSet Data Object that provides a structured and detailed classification system for categorizing various goods or products within an enterprise. It includes comprehensive classifications such as categories, subcategories, attributes, product codes, and descriptions, facilitating efficient organization, retrieval, and standardization of product information. This metadata is integral to supply chain management, effective data analysis, compliance, reporting, and seamless integration with other enterprise systems like ERP and CRM, thereby enhancing overall operational efficiency and decision-making processes.
