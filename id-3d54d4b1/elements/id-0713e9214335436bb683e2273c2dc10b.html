Goods metadata ()
Definition: Goods Metadata ABB is a DataSet Data Object that encapsulates detailed information about goods or products within an organization's system. This metadata typically includes data attributes such as product name, SKU (Stock Keeping Unit), manufacturer details, specifications, pricing, availability, and relevant classifications. It serves as a crucial element for effective management of product information, enabling accurate tracking, inventory management, and seamless integration with various business processes like sales, procurement, and supply chain operations. This data object is instrumental in enhancing data consistency, facilitating efficient retrieval and analysis of product information, and ensuring that business decisions are informed by comprehensive and up-to-date product details.
