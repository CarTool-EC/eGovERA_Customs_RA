skos:definitionAn Authorised Economic Operator accreditation digital service is an online platform that facilitates the application for, and management of, AEO status, which is an internationally recognized quality mark indicating that a company's role in the international supply chain is secure and has customs control procedures that meet EU standards.
eira:definitionSourceEuropean Commission - Taxation and Customs Union
eira:definitionSourceReferencehttps://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/dds2/eos/aeo_consultation.jsp?Lang=en
skos:exampleA logistics company uses the AEO digital service to submit their application for AEO status, including all necessary documentation and information about their security measures and compliance procedures. An exporter regularly checks the status of their AEO application through the digital service dashboard, receiving updates on the progress and any additional information requested by customs authorities. A manufacturer with existing AEO status uses the digital service to manage their renewal process, ensuring they continue to meet the criteria and maintain their AEO benefits without interruption.
skos:noteThe AEO accreditation digital service is designed to streamline the process for businesses seeking to obtain AEO status. It typically includes features such as online application forms, document submission, status tracking, and communication tools for interacting with customs authorities. The service may also provide educational resources on AEO requirements and benefits, as well as updates on relevant customs regulations. By using this digital service, companies can more efficiently manage their AEO accreditation, maintain compliance with customs regulations, and potentially benefit from simplified customs procedures and reduced inspection rates at borders.
