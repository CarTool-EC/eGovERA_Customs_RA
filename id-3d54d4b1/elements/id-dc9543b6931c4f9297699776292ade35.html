Customs statistical metadata on goods imported ()
Definition: Customs Statistical Metadata on Goods Imported ABB is a data object encompassing comprehensive descriptors and classifications of imported goods for customs purposes. This metadata includes detailed information such as the type, quantity, valuation, origin, and destination of goods, which is essential for ensuring legal compliance, facilitating trade policy analysis, and optimizing operational processes in customs management. It is a critical component for data-driven decision-making, enabling efficient customs operations and effective integration with other enterprise systems related to import activities.
