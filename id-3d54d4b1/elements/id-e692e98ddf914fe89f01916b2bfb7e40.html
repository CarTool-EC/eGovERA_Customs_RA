Risk metadata ()
Definition: Risk Metadata ABB is a Data Object that encompasses data that identifies, assesses, and categorizes various risks associated with an organization's operations or processes. This includes information on the nature of the risk, probability of occurrence, potential impact, risk mitigation measures, and monitoring strategies. This data object is crucial for effective risk management, aiding in decision-making processes, and ensuring the organization's resilience and compliance with regulatory standards.
