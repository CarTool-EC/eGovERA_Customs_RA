Economic operators metadata ()
Description: Economic Operators Information refers to the metadata related to businesses or individuals who are involved in the supply chain of goods and services. This information can include details about their legal status, financial capacity, abilities, qualifications, and reliability. It is often used in procurement processes to assess the suitability of potential suppliers, contractors, or service providers. In the context of information systems, it can be a critical component for decision-making, risk assessment, and compliance checks.
