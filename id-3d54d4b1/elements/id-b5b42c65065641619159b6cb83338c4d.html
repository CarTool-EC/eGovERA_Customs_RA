Intra-EU purchases and sales metadata ()
Definition: Intra-EU Purchases and Sales Metadata is a Data Object that refers to the collection and organization of detailed information pertaining to the buying and selling of goods and services within the European Union. It includes transaction details such as the nature of the goods or services, transaction values, dates, involved parties (buyer and seller details), VAT (Value Added Tax) information, and any relevant trade or regulatory compliance data. This metadata is key for ensuring compliance with EU trade regulations, facilitating accurate financial reporting, and enabling efficient cross-border transaction management.
