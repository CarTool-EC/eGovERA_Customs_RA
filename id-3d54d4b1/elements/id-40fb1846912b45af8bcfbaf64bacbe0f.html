BTI Application Form ()
Description: The BTI application form is used to request an official binding decision from customs authorities regarding the classification of specific goods under the Harmonized System (HS). The form collects information about the applicant, including their details and supporting documentation about the goods, such as their description, technical specifications, and intended use. The applicant suggests an HS code and provides a reason for seeking the binding decision. The completed form is submitted to the customs authority for review, and once a determination is made, the binding tariff information is communicated to the applicant, providing legal certainty for the goods' tariff classification.
