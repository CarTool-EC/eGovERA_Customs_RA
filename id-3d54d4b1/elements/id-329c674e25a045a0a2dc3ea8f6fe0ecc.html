EORI - Economic Operators Registration and Identification number ()
Description: EORI - Economic Operators Registration and Identification number provides access to the information related to the Economic Operator Identification and Registration system (EORI) as established by Council Regulation (EEC) No 2913/92 of 12 October 1992, establishing the Community Customs Code, last amended by Regulation (EC) No 648/2005 of European Parliament and of the Council of 13 April 2005.
